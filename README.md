# chasqui

**Automated customer attention over WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger** — one
inbox, consistent answers, handoff to a human when it matters.

*A chasqui was a relay messenger of the Inca road system, carrying messages — and
the quipus — across the empire.*

**English** · [Español](#español)

---

## What it is

`chasqui` answers and routes conversations across the channels where customers
actually are — WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger — so a small team can respond fast,
consistently, and around the clock, and hand off to a person when the conversation
needs one.

## Who it's for

Businesses whose customers reach them by message, not email, and who are losing
conversations to the gap between "message received" and "someone replied".
